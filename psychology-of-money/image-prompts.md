## Global style string (already baked into every prompt below)
`cinematic photograph, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, warm ochre and muted teal grade, soft practical light, fine film grain, 16:9, no text anywhere in frame, no brand logo, no readable faces (shoot backs of heads, silhouettes or hands), Indian context unless stated`

## 3 rules that protect you in production
1. **No words on images.** Every on-screen line from the script (`[TEXT]` cards) should be added in the edit as real text, not baked into AI images — otherwise you get typos you can't fix.
2. **No identifiable real people.** Read, Fuscone, Gates, Gupta are all described as *type* shots (old janitor, bankrupt mansion, 1968 computer room, arrested executive from behind). This keeps the video copyright/defamation-safe.
3. **No brand marks** — the BMW beat is shot as "a generic dark luxury German sedan".
