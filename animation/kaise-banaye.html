Sheru wali animation — kaise banegi

Right taraf wali jaan sirf SVG + 6 line ki animation hai. Neeche har step copy-paste ke saath.

Ye live SVG hai — scroll mat karo, bas dekho. Frame rate browser apne aap decide karta hai.

1 · Character SVG banao (ya reuse karo)

Har hissa ek alag <g> me ho, aur usse pehle ek <!-- naam --> comment. Bas itna kaafi hai — mera parser inhi comments se part ke naam banata hai (Head, Left Ear, Tail, Left Eye ...).

<svg viewBox="0 0 200 240">
  <!-- Tail -->
  <path d="M 145 130 Q 170 120 175 90" stroke="#D4A574"
        stroke-width="18" fill="none" stroke-linecap="round"/>
  <!-- Left Eye -->
  <circle cx="85" cy="70" r="6" fill="#333"/>
</svg>

2 · Motion = pivot + transform

Animation ka poora raaz: kisi point (pivot) ke around rotate/scale karo aur use time ke saath badlo. SVG me ye ek line hai — values me last do number pivot (cx cy) hote hain.

<animateTransform attributeName="transform" type="rotate"
  values="-16 145 132; 16 145 132; -16 145 132"
  dur="0.42s" repeatCount="indefinite"/>

3 · Blink, breathing, mouth

Blink = eye ko y-axis pe 0.08 tak squish. Breathing = body ko niche ke center se scale. Bark = mouth/tongue ko thoda neeche khicho. keyTimes se motion ka 'snap' aata hai.

<!-- blink: 4.1s me ek baar, jaldi -->
<animateTransform attributeName="transform" type="scale"
  values="1 1;1 1;1 0.08;1 1;1 1"
  keyTimes="0;0.40;0.45;0.50;1" dur="4.1s" repeatCount="indefinite"/>

<!-- breathing: 0 100 225 = feet wala pivot -->
<animateTransform attributeName="transform" type="scale"
  values="1 1;1.03 0.97;1 1" dur="3.2s" repeatCount="indefinite"/>

4 · Voiceover + caption

Script ko scene me todo (mere hisaab se aapke 5.wav…13.wav bhi ek-scene-ek-file hain). Per scene ek clip export karo: mono, 44.1 kHz, RMS ≈ −20 dBFS, koi music stem ke andar nahi.

ffmpeg -i voice_raw.wav -ac 1 -ar 44100 \
  -af "highpass=f=80,loudnorm=I=-19:TP=-1" \
  14.wav

5 · Video me bake karo

Frames -> MP4. Python script har frame par rig values recompute karti hai, isliye caption ka karaoke audio ki progress se sync rehta hai.

python3 make_animation.py --all
#  -> cute_dog_animation_9x16.mp4 (1080x1920, 30fps)
#  -> cute_dog_animation_16x9.mp4 (blur-fill reflow)

Rule of thumb: 0.4–0.5s = excited/fast, 2.5–4s = calm/loop. Ek loop me 3 values do (start; extreme; start) warna jump dikhega.

Phone-only route (bina Python)
1) Canva me character banao, parts alag PNG me export (head, ear, tail, eyes). 2) CapCut → canvas 1080×1920 → image ko timeline me daalo → Keyframe: Rotation 0°→6°→0° (2.6s) head par; Scale Y 100%→8% (0.15s) eyes par = blink. 3) Voiceover record/TTS → Auto captions → Music −18 dB + audio ducking. 4) Export 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264, 8–12 Mbps.
