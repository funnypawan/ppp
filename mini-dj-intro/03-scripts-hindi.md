# 📜 Scripts (Hindi) — Mini DJ Style Channel

4 script diye hain. Tum apne channel ka naam `MINI DJ` ki jagah apna naam rakh lo (jaise "RC DJ WALA", "Mini Dj Mayank").
Har line ke neeche `[...]` me **Roman transliteration** hai — voice artist ya TTS me daalne ke liye.

# 🅰️ SCRIPT A — 5 Second Channel Bumper (koi dialogue nahi)

**Use kab:** har video ke 15-20 second ke baad (hook ke baad).
**Kya chahiye:** logo text + 4 fast shots + sound logo.

| Time | Visual | Sound | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-0.6s | Speaker cone ka macro, LED jal rahi | Riser (deep whoosh) | — |
| 0.6-1.2s | Pickup truck ka tyre ghoomta hua (close) | Tyre screech (short) | — |
| 1.2-1.8s | DJ console ke knobs ghoomte hue | DJ scratch (2 beats) | — |
| 1.8-2.4s | Monkey bhai haath uthata hua | Sub-bass drop (BOOM) | — |
| 2.4-4.2s | **Logo reveal**: "MINI DJ" 3D text, glow, sparks | Riser + BOOM + crowd cheer | **MINI DJ** + chhota tagline: *"Ghar par banao, DJ banao"* |
| 4.2-5.0s | Logo fade + truck drive away | Music starts (video ka main BGM) | — |

# 🅱️ SCRIPT B — 15 Second Intro (Recommended ⭐)

**Use kab:** mahine me 2-3 baar, ya jab channel pe naye log aaye.
**Structure:** 4 shot + 3 dialogue + logo.

| Time | Shot | Visual (kya dikhega) | Dialogue / VO | Sound & Text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-1.2s | S1 | Black screen se DJ light ON hoti hai, neon magenta glow | *(koi dialogue nahi)* | **Scratch + bass thump** |
| 1.2-4.0s | S2 | 7 saal ka baccha, aankhein badi, LED ki roshni me baat karta hai | **बच्चा:** "पापा! आज मेरा बर्थडे है, मुझे नया मिनी डीजे चाहिए!" <br> *[Papa! aaj mera birthday hai, mujhe naya mini DJ chahiye!]* | Soft bell/music bed. Text: **"आज बर्थडे है 🎂"** |
| 4.0-6.5s | S3 | Papa sar pe haath, confusion face | **पापा:** "नया डीजे? बेटा, पैसे कहाँ से लाऊँ?" <br> *[Naya DJ? beta, paise kahan se laaun?]* | Comedy "dhin" sound. Text: **"पैसे नहीं 😅"** |
| 6.5-9.0s | S4 | Phone screen: "MAYANK BHAI CALLING 📞" + ring animation | **बच्चा:** "तो मयंक भाई से बनवा दो ना!" <br> *[To Mayank bhai se banwa do na!]* | **Phone ring** (classic Nokia tone) + dialogue |
| 9.0-11.5s | S5 | Mayank bhai workshop me, cardboard truck utha ke camera dekhta hai, smile | **मयंक भाई:** "मिनी डीजे चाहिए? चलो, आज बनाते हैं!" <br> *[Mini DJ chahiye? chalo, aaj banate hain!]* | **Whoosh + riser shuru** |
| 11.5-13.5s | S6 | **Logo reveal** — MINI DJ 3D text, speakers, confetti, truck | *(koi dialogue)* | **Sub-drop BOOM** + crowd cheer + scratch |
| 13.5-15.0s | S7 | 4-shot flash montage: speaker → light → amplifier → DJ bajta hua | *(koi dialogue)* | Music drop chalu → video ka main content |

> 🔥 **Pro tip:** Shot S2 me bacche ki aankhon me LED ki reflection zaroor rakho — log scroll rok dete hain.

# 🅲 SCRIPT C — 30 Second Story Intro (bilkul us video jaisa)

**Use kab:** jab video pehli baar koi naya "chapter" shuru kare, ya Shorts-series ke start me.
**Characters:** बच्चा, पापा, मयंक भाई, मंकी भाई, डॉगी भाई

| Time | Visual | Dialogue / VO | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0-2.0s | Dark room, baccha purane mini DJ ke paas baith hai, uski light flicker karti hai aur band ho jaati hai | **बच्चा:** "अरे पापा! मेरा मिनी डीजे तो पुराना हो गया, बंद हो गया!" | DJ power-down sound (slow pitch drop) |
| 2.0-4.5s | Papa frame me aate hain, purse kholte hain, makkhi udti hai 😅 | **पापा:** "बेटा, इस महीने बजट खत्म है। नया डीजे तो महँगा आता है।" | Comedy violin/whistle |
| 4.5-7.0s | Baccha jump karta hai, उँगली utha ke idea deta hai | **बच्चा:** "पापा! मयंक भाई को फोन करो ना — वो कार्डबोर्ड से बना देते हैं!" | **Idea "ting!"** sound |
| 7.0-9.0s | Phone close-up, ring hote hue "MAYANK BHAI" dikhta hai (2-3 ring cycle) | *(ring tone)* + **मयंक भाई (phone se):** "हेलो?" | **Nokia ring** |
| 9.0-12.0s | Papa phone pe baat karte hue, background me baccha excited | **पापा:** "मयंक भाई, छोटे के बर्थडे के लिए एक मिनी डीजे बना दो — ट्रक पर!" <br> **मयंक भाई:** "बर्थडे है? तो आज डबल स्पीकर लगेगा! पाँच मिनट में आता हूँ।" | Dialogue + soft music bed |
| 12.0-14.0s | **Cut to black, ek beat gap** | *(silence)* | 1 sec silence — फिर **SCRATCH!** |
| 14.0-18.0s | Mayank bhai workshop me: cardboard sheets, speakers ki pahaad, truck, LED lights — fast 4 cuts | **मयंक भाई:** "चलो, मिनी डीजे बनाते हैं — ट्रक पर, लाइट के साथ!" | **Riser + music build** |
| 18.0-22.0s | Speaker uthata hua, side me rakhta hua, amplifier board lagata hua (macro shots) | *(VO energy)* "ओए! कितने मस्त स्पीकर हैं!" | **Bass hit** har prop pe |
| 22.0-25.0s | **Logo reveal** — MINI DJ + pickup + sparks | **VO (deep):** "मिनी डीजे" | **Sub-drop + cheer + scratch** |
| 25.0-30.0s | Final: DJ bajta hua, lights chalti hui, monkey bhai naachta hua, "SUBSCRIBE" pop | **VO:** "आज बनाएँगे — मंकी भाई का बर्थडे डीजे!" + **मंकी भाई:** "बजाओ बजाओ!" | Music drop → मुख्य video |

# 🅳 SCRIPT D — Full Episode Beat Sheet (7-8 min video, us video ke format me)

Yeh tumhare asli video ka dhaancha hai (jaise 961K views wali video thi).

| Beat | Time | Content | Zaroori detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Hook / Demand** | 0:00-0:25 | Baccha purana DJ dikha ke naya demand karta hai | Purana DJ **actually band ho raha ho** (sound design) |
| **2. Call gag** | 0:25-0:50 | Mayank bhai ko phone, option batana (pickup / truck / e-rickshaw) | Har option ka **cutaway shot** |
| **3. Vehicle select** | 0:50-1:40 | Pickup choose, nikal ke laata hai, dust blow | Hero shot low angle |
| **4. Speaker loading** | 1:40-2:50 | 4-6 speakers uthata hai, hilate hue, side me rakhta hai | Har speaker pe "dhin" SFX |
| **5. Lights** | 2:50-3:40 | LED strip, DJ laser ko truck pe chipkata hai, test ON | Light test pe "wow" VO |
| **6. Amplifier + wiring** | 3:40-4:40 | Amplifier board lagana, wiring, knob ghumana | Macro shots, wire ka sound |
| **7. Problem! (sabse zaroori)** | 4:40-5:40 | Generator kharab / speaker fuse / wire cut | **Yahi retention twist hai** |
| **8. Doggy bhai rescue** | 5:40-6:20 | Phone call → Doggy bhai toolbox + naya part laata hai | Entry pe comedy sound |
| **9. Final assembly** | 6:20-7:00 | Sab kuch fit, screw, tape, LED glow test | Trucks ka wheel ghoomna dikhado |
| **10. Test + payoff** | 7:00-7:40 | DJ bajta hai, lights dance karti hain, monkey bhai naachta hai, baccha khush | Music **actual beat drop** pe video ka music |
| **11. End card** | 7:40-8:00 | Sab wave karte hue, subscribe pop, next video tease | "कल बनाएँगे …" — hook chhodo |

> 💡 **Beat 7 kabhi na hatao.** Us video me generator kharab hona + doggy bhai ka aana hi wo twist tha jisne log ko end tak roka.
