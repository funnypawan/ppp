# 🧪 Image generate karte waqt 8 practical tips

1. **Ek time pe ek style** — ek hi chat/session me saari images banao.
2. **Seed lock** karo (Leonardo/Midjourney me `--seed 12345`) — variation kam hoga.
3. Face theek na aaye to **img2img** karo, text prompt se dobara mat shuru karo.
4. Hands/ungli sabse zyada kharab aate hain — prompt me `hands out of frame` likh do.
5. Text wali image kabhi na banao — AI "MINI DJ" ko "MIMI OJ" bana deta hai.
6. Prompts me **light source** zaroor likho (`from below`, `rim light`, `back light`) — depth aati hai.
7. **8k / high detail / macro** words se quality badhti hai.
8. Ek hi image ke 2-3 variation banao — best wala keyframe chuno, kyunki ek image se poora 4-sec clip banega.
